Turning your super into income

When you stop work, your super needs to pay you regularly. There are three common ways, and many people combine them.

A lump sum

Take some or all of your super as cash. You decide how to spend it, but it’s up to you to make it last.

A flexible income stream

Your money stays invested and you choose your payments. It can rise or fall with markets, and the balance can run out.

A lifetime income

Regular payments for as long as you live, however long that is. It gives certainty, but it’s less flexible and you often can’t take the money back out as a lump sum.

The Age Pension can sit alongside any of these if you’re eligible.
